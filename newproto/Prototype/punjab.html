THE LAND & ITS STORY

Punjab at a Glance

Explore the identity of Punjab through its history, heritage and living traditions.

OVERVIEW

Where history lives through tradition.

Punjab, meaning “land of five rivers”, is known for its rich historical legacy, Sikh heritage, vibrant folk traditions, agricultural landscape, craftsmanship and distinctive cuisine.

Its cities and heritage sites tell stories of empires, freedom movements, spiritual traditions and community life.

THE SPIRIT OF PUNJAB

Culture & Traditions

Living traditions that make Punjab instantly recognisable.

🥁

Bhangra

Energetic folk dance closely associated with Punjab and harvest celebrations.

💃

Giddha

A lively women's folk dance expressed through singing, clapping and gestures.

🍲

Langar

A community kitchen tradition built around equality, service and sharing food.

🎵

Folk Music

Dhol, tumbi, algoza and traditional singing keep Punjab's musical heritage alive.

🌾

Harvest

Baisakhi celebrates the harvest and has deep cultural and historical importance.

🛕

Sikh Heritage

Gurdwaras, community service and sacred traditions are central to Punjab's identity.
